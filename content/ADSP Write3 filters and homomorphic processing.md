# ADSP Write3 filters and homomorphic processing

Write3 分成兩段：先看常見 filters，再進入 [[Homomorphic signal processing]] 與 [[Cepstrum]]。前半段的 filters 偏應用導向，後半段則把 convolution 透過 log transform 變成 addition。

## Concepts

- Popular filters: [[Notch filter]], [[Smoother filter]], [[Hilbert transform filter]], [[Edge detection filter]], [[Matched filter]], [[Wiener filter]], [[Equalizer filter]]
- Homomorphic processing: [[Homomorphic signal processing]], [[Cepstrum]], [[Complex cepstrum]], [[Differential cepstrum]], [[Mel-frequency cepstrum]]
- Applications: echo removal, speech analysis, multipath analysis, image/audio preprocessing
