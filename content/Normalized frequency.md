# Normalized frequency

Normalized frequency 把 physical frequency $f$ 除以 sampling rate $f_s$，或用 $\omega=2\pi f/f_s$ 表示。Nyquist/folding frequency 是 $f_s/2$，也就是 $\omega=\pi$。
